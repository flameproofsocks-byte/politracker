How is the interest score worked out?

Each bill gets 0–100 points. Hover a score to see its breakdown.

Close vote ({{ weights.close }})How narrow the closest passage vote was. A tie is full marks, a unanimous vote is zero.
Party-line ({{ weights.partisan }})How far apart Democrats and Republicans were on passage (both chambers pooled).
Amendments ({{ weights.amended }})How many amendments were filed (log-scaled, so the first few count most).
Roll calls ({{ weights.votes }})How many recorded votes the bill needed: cloture, amendments, motions.
Cosponsors ({{ weights.cosponsors }})How many members signed on as cosponsors (log-scaled).
Became law ({{ weights.law }})Signed into law.

Congress.gov publishes a “most viewed bills” list, but it can't be read automatically (the site blocks bots), so view counts aren't used here. You can still open it in your browser. A bill's date is its latest final-passage vote (or its latest vote if it never had one). The weights live in politracker/interest.py.
